## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, deterministic) writes one complete
story, told in exactly six words, to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The story

> She waited fifty years. He arrived.

## Why this story

Six words, one whole life. The first sentence is the setup — fifty
years of waiting, a lifetime spent on one hope — and the second is
the turn: he arrived. The story lands on arrival, not on the wait,
so the ending surprises after the long patience the opening builds.
Every word earns its place: "fifty years" is the scale of the wait,
"arrived" is the only word that can end it. What it leaves unsaid is
the rest of the story — who he is, why she waited, what happens now
that he is finally here — and the reader fills that in.

The story is original, written for this entry.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (35 Unicode characters)
```
